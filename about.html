Why PrintConnect?

Research behind
the idea.

Our Business Analytics study surveyed 35 students on printing habits, costs, and interest in an on-campus option.

94.3%

33 of 35 surveyed students currently print at off-campus shops.

97.1%

34 of 35 named high printing prices as a frustration.

100%

All 35 survey respondents indicated they were likely or very likely to use an on-campus service.

Source: PrintConnect business feasibility study, survey of 35 students. These sample results are not claims about all students.

Our mission

Keep academic printing within reach.

Make document printing simple, transparent, and affordable while reducing unnecessary time spent traveling outside campus for urgent requirements.

Our vision

A smoother campus experience.

Give students an approachable on-campus service, with digital price estimation and well-organized print station operations once approved and established.
