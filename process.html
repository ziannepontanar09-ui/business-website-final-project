Common questions

Good to know
before you print.

PrintConnect is a proposed business in our feasibility study. The prototype demonstrates how software could support students and staff.

Estimate your cost ↗
Can I actually submit an order here?

Not yet. This is an academic prototype with a working browser-based quotation form. There is no server, payment gateway, or operational printer connected to it.

Does the site upload or save my file?

No. If you select a local file, the browser only shows its filename. The file is never transmitted or stored by PrintConnect.

Can I print more than one copy?

Yes. Enter a number of copies from 1 to 99, and the PyScript calculator multiplies your page count by the number of copies and price per page.

Why are scanning and photocopying not in the calculator?

Our feasibility study does not establish final separate prices for those services. We show them as planned kiosk offerings rather than inventing unsupported charges.

Does it need a database?

No. HTML/CSS builds the interface, while PyScript runs Python calculations and form interactions directly in the browser. Internet is needed initially to load the PyScript runtime.
